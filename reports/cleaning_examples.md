## CASE_001 - K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India

### Before

```text
K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India

Court: Supreme Court of India
Year: 2017

Facts:
The case concerned whether privacy is protected as a fundamental right.

Issues:
Whether the right to privacy is protected under Article 21 of the Constitution of India.

Arguments:
The petitioners argued that privacy is essential to life and personal liberty.

Reasoning:
The Court examined earlier constitutional decisions and expanded the meaning of liberty.

Decision:
The Supreme Court held that the right to privacy is a fundamental right under Article 21.
```

## CASE_002 - Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India

### Before

```text
Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India

Court: Supreme Court of India
Year: 1978

Facts:
The petitioner challenged the impounding of her passport.

Issues:
Whether procedure established by law under Article 21 must be fair, just, and reasonable.

Arguments:
The petitioner argued that personal liberty cannot be restricted by arbitrary procedure.

Reasoning:
The Court expanded the interpretation of Article 21.

Decision:
The Court held that any law affecting personal liberty must be fair, just, and reasonable.
```

## CASE_003 - A.K. Gopalan v. State of Madras

### Before

```text

Court: Supreme Court of India
Year: 1950

Facts:

Issues:
Whether preventive detention violated fundamental rights.

Arguments:

Reasoning:

Decision:
```
